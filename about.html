ABOUT

Built to make scholarship searching less overwhelming.

OUR GOAL

Useful before impressive.

Scholarship Finder is an independent project that organizes scholarship opportunities into one searchable place so students can spend less time digging through scattered pages.

HOW WE VERIFY

Official sources first.

We prioritize government, university, and official scholarship-provider sources. Important fields such as eligibility, funding, deadlines, and application routes are checked before they are treated as verified.

COMMUNITY

Help keep information current.

Users will be able to report outdated deadlines, eligibility changes, broken links, or other issues. Reports are reviewed before changes are added to the public catalog.

IMPORTANT

We do not process applications.

Scholarship Finder does not provide scholarships or make admission decisions. The linked official source is always the final authority for the current application.
